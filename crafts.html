Regional Specialties

The finest examples of Malaysian craftsmanship, categorized by their states of origin.

Kelantan

Wau Bulan

The 'Moon Kite' is a symbol of Kelantanese grace, intricately hand-cut from layers of paper and bamboo.

Terengganu

Hand-Drawn Batik

Using a canting tool and molten wax, Terengganu artists create wearable masterpieces on silk and cotton.

Sarawak

Pua Kumbu

A sacred ceremonial cloth of the Iban people, woven using the complex ikat technique with natural dyes.
